Website Design Services in South Africa and Bespoke Web Development in Pretoria

Bazilburger's Web Magic builds practical websites for businesses that need to look credible online, explain their offer clearly, and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

The service is structured around three levels: a straightforward basic website or landing page for businesses across South Africa, a quoted advanced website build for South African businesses that need more functionality, and small-scale bespoke web or app development for Pretoria-based projects that need more custom planning.

Basic Website or Landing Page: R3000 Setup

This option is designed for businesses that need a clean, focused online presence without unnecessary complexity. It is the right fit if you need a basic website or landing page set up professionally and launched quickly.

Features:

  • Customized design tailored to your brand identity.
  • Responsive layout for seamless viewing on all devices.
  • Contact form integration to capture leads and inquiries.
  • Basic on-page SEO optimization for improved visibility.

Pricing:

  • R3000 once-off for design, development, and initial setup.
  • R100 per month after setup for hosting.
  • Maintenance and content changes are quoted separately depending on the amount of work required.

Advanced Websites: Quote First

This option is for websites that need more functionality than a basic informational site. That can include systems that rely on a database, such as bookings, client handling, or ecommerce website development.

Features:

  • Booking or enquiry systems that manage client information.
  • Ecommerce functionality for selling products or services online.
  • Database-backed features that require more planning and setup.
  • Quoted scope based on the business requirements of the project.

Pricing:

  • Quoted first after reviewing the required functionality, complexity, and scope.

Bespoke Small-Scale Development

This option is for smaller bespoke projects that need custom planning and development beyond a standard business website. That can include custom web-based tools or small-scale web applications where a more tailored build is required. Because the work can vary substantially, the project starts with a meeting to discuss what needs to be built and how large the project really is. This bespoke development service is specifically targeted at Pretoria.

Features:

  • Custom-built website functionality, web-based tools, or small-scale app development shaped around a specific business need.
  • Project discussion upfront to understand technical and business scope.
  • Quoted pricing after a meeting and review of the required work.
  • Referral to a larger recommended company if the project is better suited to a bigger team.

Pricing:

  • Custom quote after an initial meeting to discuss the project, requirements, and scale.
