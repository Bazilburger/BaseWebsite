Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bazilburger's Web Magic collects through this website, how that information is used, and how to contact us about it.

Information We Collect

This website is primarily an informational business website. We only collect personal information that you choose to send to us directly.

When you use the contact form, we collect the information you submit, which currently includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your selected service level
  • How you heard about us, if you choose to share it
  • Your message

If you contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp instead of using the form, we receive the information you choose to share through those channels as well.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Discuss requested services or quotations
  • Understand which service levels people are enquiring about most often
  • Understand how people are finding the business
  • Follow up on messages you send through the website
  • Protect the contact form against spam and abuse

Contact Form Processing

Messages submitted through the contact form are processed by our website server and sent to our business email address so we can review them and respond.

To help reduce spam, the contact form includes basic anti-abuse protections such as a hidden honeypot field and simple rate limiting.

Cookies and Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the site, which pages are viewed, and how people find the business.

Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, device and browser details, approximate location data, referral source, and general interaction data. This information is used in aggregated form to help improve the website and understand which services people are interested in.

This website is not set up for advertising tracking or remarketing at this time. The analytics setup is intended for website traffic and performance insights only.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits and interactions. If you block cookies in your browser, some of this analytics measurement may not function as intended.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share information where reasonably necessary to operate our communications, host the website, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Retention

We keep contact enquiries only for as long as needed to respond, continue relevant discussions, maintain business records, or meet legal and operational obligations.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no website or email transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

If you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent to us, contact us using the details below and we will respond as reasonably possible.

Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party platforms or messaging services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@bazilburger.co.za.
